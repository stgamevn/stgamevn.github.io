1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. The Game does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or address.

Information collected automatically

  • Device & technical data: device model, operating system version, language, region, screen size, and app version.
  • Advertising identifiers: such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), where permitted, used to serve and measure advertising.
  • Gameplay & usage data: levels played, progress, in-app events, session length, and performance/crash diagnostics, used to improve the Game.
  • Approximate, non-precise data: a coarse region derived from your IP address for analytics and ad compliance. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Information you choose to provide

  • Support requests: if you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message so we can respond.
  • Purchases: if you buy an optional in-app item, the transaction is processed by Apple or Google. We receive a confirmation of the purchase but not your full payment card details.

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game and its features.
  • To save your game progress and settings on your device.

  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and to comply with legal obligations.

Opting out of personalized ads does not remove advertising from the Game; it means the ads you see may be less relevant to you.
