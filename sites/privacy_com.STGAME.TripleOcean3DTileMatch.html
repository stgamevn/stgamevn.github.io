We are dedicated to maintaining a privacy-friendly gaming environment. We do not require you to provide personally identifiable information (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number) to play the core game.

When you play the Game, certain non-personal device information may be automatically collected by our third-party service providers (such as game engines, analytics, and advertising networks):

All in-app purchases (such as coin packs or booster bundles) are processed directly by Apple Inc. (App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play Store). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card or financial billing information.

The information collected is used strictly for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Game Balancing & Updates: Analyzing level difficulty, completion rates, and power-up usage to deliver fun and balanced puzzle updates.
  • In-Game Advertisements: Serving non-intrusive banner, interstitial, or rewarded video ads (such as earning coins or extra dock slots), compliant with applicable privacy standards.
